## Core Concepts

- **White line** = intraday price line (分时价格线)
- **Yellow line** = intraday average price line (分时均价线)
- Price oscillating freely around yellow line without following broader market = main force controlling the stock (有主力资金在控盘、维护)
- Long-term horizontal consolidation = waiting for the right moment to pull to limit-up (等待拉升涨停板的时机)

---

## Identifying Main Force Accumulation

### Pull-Up Accumulation (Test Drive) — 拉升吸筹（试盘）
1. After opening, free oscillation, later pull-up amplitude > 3%. Large capital behavior positively impacting price.
2. Straight-line pull-up — rapid rise in 3-5 minutes — identifies main force.
3. Volume must expand (放量). Avoid bull traps without volume.

### End-of-Day Accumulation (尾盘抢筹)
- **High position end-of-day accumulation** → usually followed by decline next day (creates space for next day drop).
- Can also be **suppression-style accumulation** (打压式吸筹).

## Accumulation Wall Patterns

**Definition**: Main force repeatedly accumulating at a price level to prepare for later pull-up.

### Key Principles
1. Main force repeatedly accumulating in one place is for subsequent pull-up.
2. **Broken limit-up board (烂板) at low position = accumulation**; at high position = distribution.
3. Prerequisite for rise: sector has continuity (板块具有持续性).
4. **Price horizontal + continuous volume bars** = accumulation behavior.

---

## V-Shaped Maintenance

### Pattern
1. Opening direct rise and pull-up.
2. After free oscillation adjustment, sudden break below average price line.
3. Quick recovery back above average line.
4. Repeatedly stepping on average line after recovery.
5. Eventually pushes to limit-up board.

### Main Force Bullish Signal
1. Opening straight rapid pull-up, rise > 3%.
2. V-shaped maintenance (best within 5 minutes).

**Buy point**: Step back to average price line without breaking (回踩均价线不破).

## Buy and Sell Types from Order Book

### Three Buy Types (盘口买入的三种类型)
1. **Consensus push route** (一致性推动路线)
2. **Divergence-to-consensus push route** (分歧转一致推动路线)
3. **Bullish kinetic energy rebound** (做多动能反扑)

### Three Sell Types (盘口卖出的三种类型)
1. **Consensus-to-divergence** (一致转分歧)
2. **Bullish kinetic energy exhaustion** (做多动能衰竭)
3. **Bull trap** (诱多陷阱)

---

## Bull Traps (诱多陷阱)

### Three Manifestations
1. **Volume-less pull-up** (无量拉升) = bull trap.
2. **Limit-up board breaks** (涨停板破板) — first reduce position, take profits. If subsequent pull-up has shrinking volume = trap.
3. **Horizontal oscillation, eventually breaks lower edge of range, rebound fails, direct dive** (横盘震荡，击穿下沿，回抽不过直接跳水).

### Kinetic Exhaustion Signals
1. Opening straight pull-up but not reaching limit-up = reduce position point (做多动能开始衰竭).
2. Price continues new highs but volume shrinks = likely pullback (量价背离).
3. High opening oscillation with shrinking volume = eventual decline.

### Consensus-to-Divergence Signals
1. First limit-up break (reduce), second break (must reduce).
2. Repeated broken boards at low = accumulation; at high = exit.
3. Morning wave放量 + end-of-day sneak放量 = next day likely low open or跌停 (except equity plays).

## Intraday Selling Signals

### Intraday Relative Selling Points
1. **Opening straight pull-up without reaching limit-up** = reduce position.
2. **Pullback rebound to secondary high** = reduce position.
3. **Break below average price line** = reduce position.

### Daily-Level Selling Points
1. **Flat/low open + dive / high open + dive** = sell at auction (竞价卖出).
2. **Explosive volume exit** (爆量离场) — explosive volume usually means adjustment or decline.
3. **Lock-chip dive** (锁筹跳水) — once platform breaks, usually big drop.
