## Left Long Black, Right Long Red

### Purpose
Confirm washout end, pullback start signal.

### Conditions
1. **Left long black** two forms:
   - One big阴线 decline > 7%
   - Two并列阴线 combined decline > 7%, each > 3%
2. **Right long red** big阳线 next day must收阳.
3. **Decline of long阴 and rise of long阳** ≥ 7%.

### Buy Signal
Next day收阳 = buy point.

### Stop Loss
Big阳线 closing price = stop-loss line.

### Valid Positions
1. After a wave of rise, at end of horizontal consolidation.
2. During a trend rise (主升浪).

### Key Summary
- Phenomenon: left long black, right long red.
- Next day must收阳.
- Long阴线 decline, long阳线 rise ≥ 7%.
- Position determines validity.

## Attack Line (进击线)

### Concept
After main force washout ends and wants to pull up, the last K-line does a诱空 washout to shake out unstable chips before拉升.

### Characteristics
- Rapid pull-up then rapid pullback.
- High position distribution not much.
- Then weak oscillation below average price line.
- Hard to attract retail接盘 — feels like main force distributed.
- Guides retail to sell = main force诱空洗盘分时.

### Conditions
- Front has accumulation and washout behavior.
- Re-pull and break previous high or near high.

### Buy Point
Next day low open or rapid pullback after opening = excellent entry point. Often quickly catches main rise opportunity.

### Stop Loss
Day's lowest point.

## Panic Washout

### Principle
One day, intraday lower shadow breaks important support level. The larger the break amplitude, the better. The longer the lower shadow, the better. The greater the panic volume during decline, the better. Then intraday slowly pulls up, closing收 back above important support.

Represents many people choosing stop-loss exit. By normal human logic, those who panic-sold in morning won't admit mistake and buy back in afternoon — this is anti-human behavior. So end-of-day is excellent short-term entry opportunity.

### Verification Standard
Next day intraday upper shadow breaks day's closing price.

## Registration System Limit-Up Pullback

### Selection Conditions
1. First board limit-up, intraday price stands on blue average price line.
2. Turnover rate > 3%.
3. Market cap within 30 billion.

### Emotional Release Point
Day's limit-up highest to lowest二分之一 position.

### Support Level
Day's limit-up lowest price.

### Entry Condition
Callback 3 days or more,阴线 breaks emotional release point at end-of-day entry.

### Exit Condition
阴线 three times fails to break new high, end-of-day exit.

## New Dragon Saber (新屠龙刀战法)

### Technical Points
1. Individual stock low position first board limit-up, volume not expanded.
2. Next day pullback surge放量 big阴线.
3. Shrunk adjustment doesn't break first board low.
4. Big阳线放量 rises from ground, follow entry.

### Notes
1. First board is natural limit-up, not一字板.
2. Emotional release point: first board limit-up entity二分之一 position.
3. Support level is also defense level; closing price break must unconditionally exit.
4. Adjustment time: 3-5 days.

### Entry Position
Emotional release point + startup阳线.

### Exit Position
- Success: break 5-day line = exit.
- Failure: break support stop-loss (3-5 points).
