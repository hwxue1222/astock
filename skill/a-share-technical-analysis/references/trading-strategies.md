## Limit-Up Trading

### Core Questions
1. What is打板? (Selling = Buying — meaning same-price transaction at limit-up).
2. Difference between capital-operated first board and height board打板?

### Conditions for打板
1. Is there main force capital? — Big阳线, limit-up board.
2. Volume must not be expanded (normal volume) = accumulation behavior.

### Preventing打板 Failure
Watch volume — volume must not exceed recent maximum volume.

## First-to-Second Board

### Conditions (一进二条件)
1. Has main force capital (has big阳线, accumulation action).
2. Market environment cannot have low-tide behavior (sector cannot have跌停).
3. First board must have strength.
4. Buy points: auction, intraday, or打板.

### Three Buy Points (一进二的三个买点)
1. **Auction buy point** (竞价买点) —当日主流.
2. **Intraday buy point** (盘中买点) — intraday order book has stability buy point (nine types).
3. **Limit-up buy point** (打板买点) — watch volume.

## Lurking First Board

### Conditions
1. Continuous accumulation.
2. Accumulation wall present.

### Prerequisites
- First have big阳线 or limit-up board, representing stock's theme/concept is recent hot风口 — subsequent easier to explode limit-up.
- Main force操盘线 not broken, any decline can be viewed as washout! While washing, capital continuously accumulates = brief washout.
- First have big阳线 or limit-up board (represents hot theme), during washout capital continuously accumulates, finally shrunk volume represents washout has reached end, ready to pull up at any time.

### Core Points
1. Lurking first board core points.
2. Capital operation accumulation.
3. Theme hotspot.

## Institution Stocks vs. Short-Term Stocks

### Institution Stocks
Stocks with institution shareholders (public funds, north-bound capital,社保, insurance capital, etc.). During pull-up, easily form trend行情.

### Short-Term Stocks
No institution shareholders. Often main force accumulates and washes out then pulls one wave and done. Sometimes has dragon-head-return行情. Principle: follow main force, finish one wave and done.

### Operation Differences
- **Short-term stocks**: Follow main force finish one wave. Stop-loss/defense use short-term methods.
- **Institution stocks**: Principle is to finish one trend行情.止盈保护线和防守 methods must follow trend operation.

### Defense Methods for Institution Stocks
- Novice: use effective break of 60-day line as defense.
- Expert: use trend divergence forming sell point as sell.

### Example
2022 CPO行情中, many were institution stocks. Using short-term defense methods cannot capture 5x+行情.
