## 39 Volume Trading Rules

1. **High volume Day 1** = observe (观).
2. **High volume Days 2-3 above support line** = 阳上阴观 (yang above yin, observe).
3. **High volume breaks** = disaster (高量破，有灾祸) → clear position (清).
4. **Day's volume > previous 3 days' volume** = high volume (高量) = main force anomaly = inflection point = direction change.
5. **Shrink/flat volume with lower shadow** = risk; **high volume with lower shadow** = opportunity.
6. **Use day's high volume entity low as support**.
7. **Use past high volume entity high/low as pressure**.
8. **Not all high volume lower shadows are risks**.
9. **High volume defines 3 days; 3 days define nature** (高量定三天，三天定性).
10. **High volume不分阴阳; volume doesn't look at color**.
11. **Fake yin / fake yang** = observe.
12. **Rising staircase volume** = risk; opposite = opportunity.
13. **Over-top through-bottom = cycle change** = risk = observe.
14. **Downtrend with 3 intermittent high volumes** = opportunity.
15. **Within 3 days of high volume, shrunk volume long leg** = observe.
16. **Downtrend揉搓线** = risk.
17. **Upper shadow hits past pressure line and can't pass** = reduce.
18. **Downtrend shrunk/flat volume rebound** = observe.
19. **Downtrend first high volume** = bull trap = observe 3 days.
20. **Technical contradiction** = prioritize observing.
21. **Continuous 3 days lower highs and lower lows** = downtrend.
22. **Support line above with bottom分型** = upward position.
23. **Shrunk volume lower shadow next day收阴** = reduce.
24. **Support line above with red three soldiers** = upward position.
25. **Heaven and earth** = observe (shadow is 2x entity).
26. **Upper shadow broken by yang line entity** = upward position.
27. **Within 3 days of high volume, lower highs and lower lows** = reduce.
28. **Below pressure line with top分型** = reduce position.
29. **Continuous red ≥ 4 days sees green (with volume)** = reduce.
30. **High volume upper shadow broken by yang line entity** = important/re-enter.
31. **High volume breaks, next day收阳** = observe.
32. **Below pressure line green three soldiers** = reduce/clear.
33. **Ground volume ≠ ground price**.
34. **Uptrend high volume long leg** = reduce.
35. **Uptrend intermittent 3 high volumes** = risk.
36. **Continuous 3 days high volume** = staircase volume.
37. **High volume Days 2-3 above support** = 阳上阴观.
38. **High volume break easy to error** (shrunk/flat/expanded volume = clear).
39. **More lower shadows, longer lower shadows = greater decline probability**.
40. **Large volume small entity, mostly someone running** = reduce (at high position).
41. **Downtrend: shrunk/expanded/flat with lower shadow = risk; high volume = opportunity**.

## Special Volume-Price Rhymes

### 1. Accumulation Rhyme (建仓口诀)
> "Price slowly rises, volume slowly expands"
> — Slow expansion represents obvious main force accumulation.

### 2. Washout Rhyme (洗盘口诀)
> "Large volume small entity, mostly someone running"
> — Larger volume = more money spent; spending money without result won't last.

### 3. Breakthrough Rhyme (大量缩量破)
> "Shrink volume break, hold with confidence"
> — Shrunk volume with small pressure passing previous high = good washout.

### 4. Distribution Rhyme (出货口诀)
> "Large volume next day yin, clear all positions"
> — Main force distribution will definitely trap many retail investors.

### 5. Big Decline Model (大跌模型)
> "First rebound after big decline, direct position-building causes trouble"
> — After big decline rebound, trapped holders first think of selling.

### 6. Escape Top Rhyme (逃顶口诀)
> "Price doesn't pass high volume passes, entering means catching a falling knife"
> — Price not passing but volume passing means someone is urgently selling / not optimistic.

### 7. Support/Pressure Rhyme (支撑压力口诀)
> "Yang line breaks half, must switch when needed"
> — Yang line breaks opening, hard to rebound; big yang line half and opening are key pressure levels.

### 8. Gap Rhyme (缺口口诀)
> "As long as gap isn't filled, previous high will eventually pass"
